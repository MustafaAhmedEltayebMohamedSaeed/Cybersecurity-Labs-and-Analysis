# Cybersecurity Risk Register & Prioritization Analysis

## Overview
A practical risk management analysis focused on identifying, scoring, and prioritizing organizational threats within a commercial banking operational environment. Threats were evaluated using a $3 \times 3$ Risk Matrix based on Likelihood and Severity metrics to establish clear remediation priorities.

## Risk Assessment Framework
* **Risk Score Formula:** $\text{Likelihood} \times \text{Impact Severity} = \text{Priority Score}$
* **Likelihood Scale:** 1 (Low) to 3 (High)
* **Severity Scale:** 1 (Low) to 3 (High / Catastrophic)

## Key Findings & Prioritized Threats
1. **High Priority (Score: 6):**
   * **Business Email Compromise (BEC):** Employee credential phishing targeting banking funds.
   * **Database Exposure:** Poor encryption on customer databases exposing sensitive records.
   * **Financial Data Leak:** Publicly accessible database server containing financial backups.
2. **Moderate Priority (Score: 2):**
   * **Physical Theft:** Unlocked bank safe physical vulnerability.
3. **Low Priority (Score: 1):**
   * **Supply Chain Disruption:** Delivery delays due to natural disasters.

## Concepts & Skills Demonstrated
* Risk Identification & Threat Analysis
* Quantitative Risk Assessment ($3 \times 3$ Risk Matrix)
* NIST Risk Management Framework principles
* Asset Protection & Remediation Prioritization
